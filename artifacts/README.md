# Artifacts

English | [日本語](README.ja.md) | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

Generated and local research artifacts are separated by role:

- `checkpoints/`: adapter checkpoints. Local only; the entire directory is gitignored.
- `metrics/raw/`: direct experiment output. JSON preserves nested and per-item records; CSV sidecars expose
  rectangular measurements.
- `metrics/tables/`: deterministic, paper-facing CSV files produced by `tools/build_result_tables.py`.
- `logs/slurm/`: local scheduler stdout and historical diagnostics.
- `logs/evaluation/`: local diagnostic log per current evaluation program.
- `runs/`: structured submission records generated by `reproduce.sh gpu-eval`.
- `figures/`: generated visual assets, when needed.

Logs are gitignored and are not authoritative numeric datasets. Use `metrics/raw/` for public experiment records
and `metrics/tables/` for reported relations. In particular, `metrics/raw/training_curve.csv` replaces the former
dependency on site-local training logs.

`metrics/tables/paper_result_coverage.csv` is the machine-readable symmetry
audit. Every public cross-family result family must contain matched AB and BA
rows; the canonical QA matrix additionally contains AA and BB at every task
and switch layer. Pythia early exits are classified as `B_early_exit`, not
miscounted as the BB adapter control. `four_path_complete` is left empty when
AA/BB controls do not apply to that measurement family, rather than labeling a
matched AB/BA experiment as incomplete.
